Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

Overview

Carteira PIX ("the App") is a personal PIX key manager and charge generator. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with third parties. All data you enter (PIX keys, charges, settings) is stored exclusively on your device.

iCloud Sync

If you enable the iCloud sync feature, your data is stored in your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit service. This data is only accessible by you, on devices signed in to your Apple ID. We have no access to your iCloud data.

Analytics and Tracking

The App does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks. No usage data is collected or sent anywhere.

Biometric Data

If you enable biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID), the App uses Apple's LocalAuthentication framework. Biometric data is handled entirely by the operating system and is never accessed or stored by the App.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. If iCloud sync is enabled, data is additionally stored in your private CloudKit container. You can delete your data at any time from within the App or via iOS Settings.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, or SDKs.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from children. Since no personal data is collected from any user, this applies equally to all age groups.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
